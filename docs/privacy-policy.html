Privacy Policy

Application: Tic Tac Toe: Aurora Glow (studio.gameloop.tictactoe)
Effective Date: November 1, 2026

Short Summary: Tic Tac Toe: Aurora Glow is an offline game. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or store any personal information, device identifiers, or user tracking data. Your privacy is 100% protected.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application Tic Tac Toe: Aurora Glow (referred to as "the App", "we", "us", or "our"), developed and published under package name studio.gameloop.tictactoe.

We respect your privacy and are committed to maintaining a safe, transparent, and respectful experience for all players.

2. Information We Collect

We collect zero personal data. Specifically:

  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, or postal addresses.
  • No Location Data: The App does not access, monitor, or track GPS or network location.
  • No Device Information: We do not collect MAC addresses, IMEI, advertising IDs (AAID), or hardware identifiers.
  • No Biometric Data: No camera, microphone, or sensor recording permissions are requested or used.

3. Local Device Storage

The App saves basic gameplay state locally on your device, including:

  • Player scores (Player X wins, Player O wins, tied draws)
  • Current win streaks and sound/vibration preferences
  • Daily challenge progress

All of this data is stored strictly in sandboxed local application storage (Android SharedPreferences / local key-value store). It never leaves your device and is deleted if you uninstall the App or clear app data in Android settings.

4. Third-Party Services & Advertising

  • No Advertising SDKs: The App does not contain third-party ad networks (e.g. AdMob, Unity Ads, etc.).
  • No Analytics SDKs: We do not embed telemetry or third-party behavioral analytics tools (e.g. Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Facebook Pixel).
  • No Social Media SDKs: There are no social network plugins embedded within the App.

5. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & Google Play Families Policy)

The App is safe for all audiences, including children and families. In compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the Google Play Families Policy:

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area).
  • Because the App collects no personal information whatsoever, parents and guardians can be confident that children can enjoy the game safely.

6. Device Permissions

Tic Tac Toe: Aurora Glow does not require or request any sensitive runtime permissions. It does not require access to your camera, microphone, contacts, storage files, phone state, or location.
