# Privacy Policy for Tic Tac Toe: Aurora Glow

**Application:** Tic Tac Toe: Aurora Glow  
**Package Name:** `studio.gameloop.tictactoe`  
**Developer:** GameLoop Studio  
**Effective Date:** November 1, 2026  
**Hosted Web Version:** [https://ashrafkhan19.github.io/tic-tac-toe/privacy-policy.html](https://ashrafkhan19.github.io/tic-tac-toe/privacy-policy.html)

---

## 1. Summary
*Tic Tac Toe: Aurora Glow* is an offline strategy board game. **We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, device identifiers, or tracking data.** Your privacy is fully protected.

---

## 2. Information Collection and Use
We collect **no personal information**:
- **Personal Identifiers:** No names, emails, phone numbers, contacts, or addresses.
- **Location Data:** No GPS, network, or location tracking.
- **Device & Hardware Data:** No IMEI, MAC address, Advertising ID (AAID), or hardware fingerprints.
- **Sensors & Media:** No camera, microphone, or biometric data accessed.

---

## 3. Local Storage Only
The application saves gameplay state strictly on your local device:
- Score counters (Player X wins, Player O wins, draws)
- Win streak counters and sound/haptic toggles
- Daily challenge completion timestamps

All preferences and stats reside in private, sandboxed device storage (Android `SharedPreferences`). This data never leaves your device and is wiped when the app is uninstalled or app cache is cleared.

---

## 4. Advertising & Third-Party Services
The application integrates **Google AdMob** (`com.google.android.gms:play-services-ads`) to display non-intrusive banner and interstitial advertisements in the classic Tic-Tac-Toe game mode (focus and sanctuary modules remain 100% ad-free).

- **Google AdMob / Google Play Services:** Google may collect and process device identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID / AAID), IP address, and coarse diagnostic/performance telemetry in accordance with [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **No Analytics / Tracking SDKs:** We do not embed telemetry or third-party behavioral analytics tools (e.g., Firebase Analytics, Adjust, Facebook Pixel).
- **No User Account Data:** No login, profile, or contact information is collected or sent to our servers.

---

## 5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Families Policy)
In compliance with the **Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)** and **Google Play Families Policy**:
- The application does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.
- Because no personal information is collected from any user, the application is safe for players of all ages.

---

## 6. App Permissions
The application does not request any sensitive Android runtime permissions (e.g., Camera, Location, Contacts, Microphone, External Storage).

---

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may revise this Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be reflected in this document with a new "Effective Date".
